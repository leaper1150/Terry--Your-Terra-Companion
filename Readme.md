  # Terry — Your Terra Companion

  **A desktop companion that helps you stay on track with your Hackatime goals.**
</div>

Terry is a fox who keeps an eye on your coding time through [Hackatime](https://hackatime.hackclub.com) and helps you stay on top of your daily and weekly goals. His colors change to show whether you're on track or falling behind, and he gives you a nudge when it's time to catch up.

## What Terry does

- Shows your coding time for today and this week.
- Tracks your daily and weekly goals.
- Changes color to make your progress easy to spot.
- Gives you a reminder when you need a little motivation.

## How to use Terry

1. Launch Terry.
2. Connect your Hackatime account and choose your daily and weekly goals in the app.
3. Check Terry's color and progress to see how you're doing.
4. If Terry warns that you're falling behind, use the time remaining to plan a catch-up session.

> “I'm TERRIFIED you'll lose. Get it? I'm Terry!”

Terry is rooting for you all the way to the finish line.

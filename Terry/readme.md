# Terry — Your Terra Companion

Terry is a fox who helps keep you motivated throughout your 12-week Terra journey—and reminds you of the reward waiting at the finish line.

## What Terry does

- Connects to your [Hackatime](https://hackatime.hackclub.com/) account, with manual setup or automatic configuration.
- Keeps track of how your Terra projects are going.
- Gives you a visual reminder of your progress and how much time is left.
- Lets you know when you’re falling behind, including a ping to help you get back on track.

Terry lives on your desktop, so your progress stays in view while you work.
